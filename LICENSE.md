## Files derived from THEMol

The following files contain values derived from the THEMol dataset, which is
licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial 4.0 International
License. These files are therefore distributed under CC BY-NC 4.0:
<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/>.

- `results/tables/confirmation_representative_curves.csv`: DFT target energies
  of four representative confirmation profiles.
- `results/tables/confirmation_profile_characteristics.csv`: target barriers and
  SMILES of the 1,000 confirmation profiles.
- `results/tables/confirmation_representative_profiles.csv`: SMILES of the four
  representative molecules.
